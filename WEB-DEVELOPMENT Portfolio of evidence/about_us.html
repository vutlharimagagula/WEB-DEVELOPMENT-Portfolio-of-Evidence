About Us

The story behind Ndzalo Ra Rhulani, and who we're here to serve.

Our Story

Ndzalo Ra Rhulani was founded in 2026 by Vutlharhi Magagula, born and raised in Nelspruit, Mpumalanga. The organisation was established to address the digital divide facing local small businesses, micro-enterprises and community ventures.

Operating as a community-driven initiative, we provide accessible technical guidance and web presence support so that local entrepreneurs can navigate the modern digital economy with confidence, not confusion.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower small businesses and community initiatives in Mpumalanga by providing accessible, reliable and user-friendly digital tools, web presence solutions and ongoing online guidance.

Vision: To build a thriving digital ecosystem across Mpumalanga where every small business, regardless of size or background, can effortlessly attract opportunities and achieve sustainable growth online.

Who We Serve

Three groups make up our community — and each one has a place on this website.

Small Business Owners

Local entrepreneurs, artisans and service providers (aged 25–55) in Nelspruit seeking an affordable digital presence to reach more clients.

Sponsors & Donors

Corporate social investment (CSI) representatives and individual donors looking to support sustainable economic empowerment initiatives in Mpumalanga.

Community Volunteers & Youth

Local IT students and tech-savvy youth interested in volunteering to assist small businesses with digital setup while gaining practical field experience.

What We Value

  • Accessibility. Digital support should never be out of reach because of cost or technical jargon.
  • Community first. Every feature we build starts with a real need from a Nelspruit business owner or volunteer.
  • Sustainable growth. We aim for lasting digital habits, not one-off fixes.
